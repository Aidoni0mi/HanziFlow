### Local user material (not bundled)
Project uploads include HSK Standard Course/workbook files named for levels 1–4, plus an HSK2 grammar test. Filenames alone do not prove editions or licensing. No content extracted for this checkpoint; no claimed HSK 3.0 mapping. PDF/TXT can be imported from a user's lawfully held local copies, manually tagged `HSK 2.0`, `HSK 3.0` or `unknown`. DOCX and other import types remain pending. The CSCA math document is out of scope.

### Editorial pipeline
Record immutable source/version, checksum, dates, license and review status for each imported content pack. Separate standard-2021, HSK-exam-3.0 and legacy HSK-2.0. Deduplicate by canonical word + sense, preserving variant readings and level per curriculum. Never infer official level from word length or the old HSK list. Two-stage review: official mapping then Chinese/Russian linguistic validation. For samples and textbooks, reference permitted links or personal import, not scraping or copying.

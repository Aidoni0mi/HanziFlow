# Privacy — alpha

No app INTERNET permission, accounts, analytics SDK, advertising, cloud sync or API keys. Android external TTS engine is a separate process; only voices advertised as non-network Mandarin CN/SG are selected, but the engine's own behavior is outside this app's control. If unavailable, app does not intentionally choose network TTS.

Microphone permission requested only after a user starts recognition or recording. Up to fifteen seconds of mono PCM are retained in memory; delete button and app-process termination discard references, not guaranteed forensic zeroization. Recording job canceled on leaving speech practice or activity stopping. Playback local. Books copied to app-private storage, names/reading position in local SQLite. Delete book removes private copy, not source. Android backup disabled. Local progress retained until uninstall/clear storage. No cloud transmission implemented. No claim of formal privacy compliance or penetration testing. Future cloud processing requires explicit separate consent and retention controls.

0.3.1: explicit user-initiated on-device SpeechRecognizer on API 31+ only; no fallback to network recognition. Android speech service is an external system component, not controlled by HanziFlow. Up to five local recognition hypotheses may be returned; the app displays the closest textual match and discloses that choice. Recognition text stays in memory. One pasted or user-selected UTF-8 TXT dialogue and daily completion may be stored in private preferences; the saved dialogue can be deleted in the app and is never added to bundled content. Android backup remains disabled and the app implements no upload. Microphone permission is also requested for recognition. No claim of verified acoustic scoring.

0.4.0: life-situation texts are bundled original content and contain no personal data. Guided reading sends the selected line only to the installed Android TTS component and requests on-device Mandarin recognition; both components are outside HanziFlow's control. Recognition results and F0 samples stay in memory. The app does not save or upload life-course recordings. Speech-text similarity and the separate F0 contour are practice hints, not verified pronunciation or per-syllable tone scores.
